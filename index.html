How this works
  • Amount applied is calculated for you as number of shares × price — you only enter shares and price.
  • Profit if allotted = number of shares × GMP. Allotment is treated as binary, not prorata: you either get the full lot and this full profit, or you get nothing and the profit is zero. Every profit figure shown on this page — including the stat card and every table — is this un-averaged, full-payoff number, assuming that particular application is allotted.
  • Allotment odds is approximated as min(1, 1 ÷ subscription) — roughly your chance of winning the lottery draw at that oversubscription level. These odds are used only behind the scenes when you choose Diversify applications.
  • Funds are treated as blocked from your planned apply date through and including the allotment date, and only become free again starting the day after allotment.
  • Number of accounts lets the scheduler split your applications across separate demat accounts. A single account can choose only one category per IPO, while different accounts can independently apply — even to the same IPO/category — against one shared fund pool.
  • The Recommendations panel shows one account's queue at a time (use the dropdown when you have more than one account), numbered in the order you'd actually submit them as funds free up. Applications that don't make it into any account are shown separately as Skip.
  • The optimizer uses maximum profit if allotted in Maximize profit mode, and balances profit potential with allotment chances in Diversify applications mode. It respects the shared day-by-day fund cap and the rule that each account can choose at most one category for any IPO. Overlapping IPOs within the same account are allowed.
  • This is a decision-support estimate, not investment advice. Real allotment mechanics (lot-based draws, cut-off pricing, category re-shuffling) are simplified.
